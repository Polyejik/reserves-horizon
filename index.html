The protocol

What “verified” actually means.

Verification is a defined process, not an adjective. Every signed answer carries the same four steps and the same paper trail.

  1. 01

    Scope

    The question is bound before work starts: what decision it supports, what data is admissible, and what the answer will not cover.

  2. 02

    Review

    A specialist re-derives the result independently — checking sources, arithmetic, stated assumptions and whether the answer is physically plausible.

  3. 03

    Sign

    The answer is issued under a named engineer’s signature with the review record attached, or it is returned with the discrepancies listed.

  4. 04

    Record

    The version, the inputs and the reviewer are logged, so the answer can be reproduced and defended months later.
