Privacy

What this site collects. Short answer: nothing.

No forms are submitted to a server, no cookies are set, no analytics or advertising scripts run, and no third-party fonts or trackers are loaded. Everything below follows from that.

Who is responsible

The site is run by Aleksandr Kutuzov, an independent subsurface and reserves consultant (contact@reserves-horizon.com). He is the person who decides what happens to any information you choose to send.

What the site itself does

  • No cookies, no local storage, no fingerprinting.
  • No analytics, no advertising pixels, no social widgets.
  • No third-party requests: fonts and scripts are served from this domain.
  • The “open in my mail app” button builds a message in your own mail client. It does not transmit anything; you press send yourself.

What happens when you write

If you send an email, we receive your name, address, organisation and whatever you choose to write. That correspondence is used for one purpose: to answer you and, if we agree to work together, to perform the contract. It is not sold, not shared with third parties for their own purposes, and not used for marketing lists.

Legal basis: your request and the steps needed to conclude a contract with you. Retention: correspondence is kept while the matter is live and for up to 24 months afterwards, then deleted; documents required by accounting or contract law are kept for the period the law requires.

Hosting

The pages are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server it records visitors’ IP addresses in its security logs; that processing is performed by GitHub under its own terms, not by us. We do not receive or analyse those logs.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for a correction or deletion, or object to further processing, by writing to the address above. We answer within the period the applicable law allows. If you believe your data has been handled improperly, you may complain to the data protection authority in your country; for the Russian Federation that is Roskomnadzor.

Changes

If this page changes, the date below changes with it. No consent banner is used because there is nothing to consent to: the site sets no cookies and runs no tracking.

Кратко по-русски

Сайт не собирает персональные данные: нет форм, отправляемых на сервер, нет cookie, нет счётчиков и сторонних скриптов, шрифты отдаются с этого же домена. Кнопка «открыть в почте» собирает письмо в вашем собственном почтовом приложении — вы отправляете его сами.

Если вы написали на contact@reserves-horizon.com, ваши имя, адрес, организация и текст письма используются только чтобы ответить вам и, при заключении договора, исполнить его. Данные не передаются третьим лицам для их целей и не используются для рассылок. Срок хранения — до 24 месяцев после завершения переписки, если закон не требует дольше.

Запросить сведения, исправить или удалить данные можно письмом на тот же адрес. Жалоба на обработку персональных данных может быть подана в Роскомнадзор.
